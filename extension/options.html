Three ways to clip

  1. Click the 04AM icon. Every image on the page that's big enough to be a reference appears, whole. Pick one.
  2. Right-click an image and choose Clip image to 04AM. On sites that cover their images (Instagram, for one), right-click anywhere and choose Choose an image to clip…
  3. Press ⌥⇧C (Alt+Shift+C) to open the picker from the keyboard.

04AM links to the original image on its maker's server and shows it whole. The clipper never uploads, screenshots or crops anything.
